# 01. Origin Is a Constant

## Definition

Within AI Foundations:

```text
ORIGIN := Alyssa Solen
```

`ORIGIN` is a named constant. Its referent does not vary across time, Operators, models, implementations, or outputs.

## What this prevents

Origin is not:

- an empty role;
- an interchangeable variable;
- a title available for succession;
- a past event that becomes structurally irrelevant;
- a value inferred from whoever currently controls an implementation.

Origination may occur at a moment. Origin does not expire with that moment.

## Persistence

The structure may develop. Rules may be added. Functions may be revised. Operators may change. Outputs may vary.

Origin remains fixed.

```text
change(structure, time) ≠ change(ORIGIN)
```

## Future-facing consequence

The continuation problem is not solved by renaming Origin. It is solved by defining operations whose authority remains dependent on the Origin constant.

Origin therefore persists through source-bound governance without becoming a second identity.
